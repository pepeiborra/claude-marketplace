# Water chemistry for this pool

## The constraint everything hangs on
Calcium hardness **CH ≈ 650 ppm** (drop kit 2026-10-05, down from 750 after draining 33 cm and refilling with ~10 cm of rain; hard Dénia mains, concentrates with evaporation; always read the live value in `input_number.pool_calcium_hardness`). You cannot lower it chemically; only dilution (rain, softened top-up) does. Therefore the usual "TA 80–100, pH 7.5–7.6" advice is wrong here: keep LSI ≈ 0…+0.1 by running **TA ≈ 70 ppm (60–80)** and **pH 7.3–7.5** (controller pH max 7.4, min 7.0). `sensor.pool_lsi` (package `pool_lsi.yaml`) computes LSI live from pH, temperature, `input_number.pool_calcium_hardness` (750), TA estimate, `input_number.pool_cya` (26) and `pool_tds_factor` (12.29 for TDS ≈ 3840 measured with an Xlux TDS meter; salt ≈ 3.2 g/L). LSI = pH + TF + CF + AF − TDSF with CF = log10(CH) − 0.4 and AF = log10(TA − 0.33·CYA).

## Conversions and stoichiometry (60 m³)
- 1 dKH = 17.86 ppm CaCO₃. Salifert KH is the trusted TA test (0.1 dKH resolution); strips are ±20 ppm; the Scuba II reads TA ~50 ppm high.
- Sodium bicarbonate ("hidrogenocarbonato de sodio", food grade is fine): 1 kg → +9.9 ppm TA in 60 m³ (595 g CaCO₃-eq per kg). Self-limiting at pH 8.3 → a full dose at once is safe; pH rises transiently.
- Acids per litre, as ppm of TA destroyed in 60 m³: H₂SO₄ 33 % → 417 g CaCO₃ = 7.0 ppm/L; HCl 22 % (salfumán Josmi, current) → 335 g = 5.6 ppm/L; H₂SO₄ 10 % → 109 g = 1.8 ppm/L. The HA model (`pool_alkalinity.yaml`) integrates acid-relay runtime × `input_number.pool_acid_pump_lph` (1.5) × strength of the selected acid (`input_select.pool_acid_type`, "Agua (lavado)" = 0) into `sensor.pool_alkalinity_drained_total`; estimate = baseline − drained since baseline + bicarbonate logged. Re-baseline after every drop test: `pool/cmd/alk_baseline_ppm <ppm>`; log bicarbonate: `pool/cmd/bicarb_kg <kg>`.
- CYA: 0.5 kg → ≈ +8 ppm. Measure with a turbidity (black dot) test; the Scuba II CYA is unreliable.
- Salt: AquaRite needs ≈ 3–3.5 g/L; `LOW` salt flag stops the cell around 2.5–2.8 g/L. 1 kg salt per m³ = +1 g/L → 25 kg sack raises 60 m³ by ≈ 0.42 g/L.
- Free chlorine target = **7.5 % of CYA** (CYA 26 → ≈ 2 ppm), floor 5 % (1.3 ppm). Shock only with liquid sodium hypochlorite (never calcium hypochlorite at CH 750).

## ORP ↔ free chlorine ↔ CYA
- ORP depends on HOCl, pH and CYA (≈ −2 mV per ppm CYA). Empirical map for this pool at CYA 26, pH 7.5: 630 mV ≈ 1.3 ppm, 650 ≈ 1.5–1.8, 670 ≈ 2 ppm. Each +40 mV ≈ ×1.6 FC.
- ORP rises overnight (no UV) to 710–730 mV even with low FC, and falls to ~610 by afternoon with the cell at 100 %. Night readings overstate FC.
- Probe artefacts: with the pump OFF the probe chamber stagnates → redox can read 420–500 and pH 7.7+ — ignore readings while `binary_sensor.pool_pump_relay` is off. Scale on the redox probe reads low (HCl 1:10 clean fixed −48 → −15 mV on 2026-09-25). Probe calibration kit 468 mV; bottled low-mineral water is fine for rinsing.
- **Real vs operative redox setpoint (D14, 2026-09-30)**: `input_number.pool_redox_objetivo` = chemistry target (670 for CYA 26); `number.pool_target_redox` = what the controller runs with. The controller stops the cell whenever redox ≥ its setpoint (`ON_TARGET`), so a setpoint at 670 silently killed night production (22.6 h → 6–9 h of production/day, 500 → 150 g/day, FC fell to 1.3). `automation.pool_redox_operativa` (17:24) adjusts the operative so redox ≥ objetivo during ≥ 50 % of sun samples (10:00–17:30 every 15 min): f < 0.25 → +40 mV, f < 0.5 → +20, 0.5–0.8 hold, ≥ 0.8 → −10, clamped [objetivo, `pool_redox_operativa_max` = 790]. `sensor.pool_orp_fraccion_ok` shows f. The chlorination-hours rule (R2, 17:25) only adds hours when the operative is already at the cap. Cell at 22 g/h × pump hours is the production ceiling (16 h → 350 g ≈ 5.9 ppm/day gross).

## Decision tables

**TA measured (Salifert dKH → ppm):**
| Reading | Action |
|---|---|
| < 50 ppm (< 2.8 dKH) | Low: dose bicarbonate to 70 in ONE addition: kg = (70 − TA)/9.9. Re-baseline the model to the measured value first. Expect pH to rise ~0.1–0.2 for a day; keep acid max 7.4; re-measure in 48 h. Check why it fell: acid consumption (`sensor.pool_acid_dosed_24h`, drained 24 h), aeration (returns above water, waterfalls), rain. |
| 50–60 | Slightly low: top up to 70 (same formula), re-baseline, re-check in 3–4 days. |
| 60–80 | OK: re-baseline the model, nothing else. |
| > 90 | High for CH 750 (LSI > +0.3 at pH 7.5): let acid dosing bring it down; do not add bicarbonate; keep pH ≤ 7.4. |
Example: 3.0 dKH = 53.6 ppm → (70 − 53.6)/9.9 = **1.65 kg** bicarbonate; after adding: `pool/cmd/alk_baseline_ppm 54` then `pool/cmd/bicarb_kg 1.65` (or baseline directly at 70 once mixed, 2 h of pumping).

**pH**: controller max 7.4 / min 7.0 (since 2026-10-05 the max is **autoregulated from the live LSI**, D15: see §Real vs operative below and `scheduler.md`; the owner can switch it off with `input_boolean.pool_ph_autorregulado`). The loop only doses when pH > max + 0.1, so a reading hovering 0.05–0.10 above the max with 1-min pulses every hour or so is the normal equilibrium, not a fault; the display rounds 7.47–7.50 to "7,5". Reading pinned at 7.5 for > 24 h with pulses running → acid not arriving (see acid line) or aeration; pH 7.7–7.9 bursts happen when returns spray in air (pool half empty) — fix the cause, don't chase with acid (it destroys TA). pH 6.x readings = acid plume at the probe (manual run) or stagnation.

**Free chlorine (DPD)**: < 1.3 ppm (5 % CYA) → check production first (hours of cell producing/day via `poller_report.py`, operative setpoint, pump hours, LOW salt, FL1), then UV/CYA (CYA < 20 → add 0.5 kg), then shock with liquid chlorine if needed. 2–3 ppm OK. > 4 ppm → lower operative setpoint cap or hydrolysis %.

**CYA**: target 25–35 (less = UV burns chlorine fast; more = slower kill). After a measured change set `input_number.pool_cya`. Do NOT move the redox objetivo with CYA: the FC target follows CYA (7.5 %) precisely so that the active HOCl, and therefore ORP, stays constant — the 670 mV objetivo is valid at CYA 19 (FC ≈ 1.4) and at CYA 30 (FC ≈ 2.2). Only change the objetivo if DPD shows the FC/ORP mapping itself has shifted (probe scale, pH change) — measure DPD and ORP together to re-map.

**Salt / TDS**: after dilution events add salt to 3.2 g/L; the cell `LOW` flag means stop everything else and add salt.

## Acid dosing line (physical facts and diagnostics)
- Peristaltic pump 1.5 L/h nominal (`input_number.pool_acid_pump_lph`), relay 1 of the NeoPool, pulses ≈ 1 min ON / 5 min pause (`number.pool_acid_pulse_max` 600 s, `pool_acid_pulse_pause` 300 s), `MAX_TIME` 150 min cumulative (`number.pool_acid_max_dosing_time`), `RELAY_MODE` 2 (alarm + stop; code 3 = "pump timeout"; reset via `script.pool_ph_alarm_reset` / `NPEscape`).
- Injector on the pressure side **before the cell**; pH probe ~1 m **upstream** of the injector with two elbows. With flow, a 3-min manual run shows up at the probe as a dip of 0.03–0.5 pH within 2–4 min (recirculation); no dip at all = no acid delivered.
- The pump sits **1.8 m above the drum** with a **2 m × 4 mm suction tube (≈ 25 mL)**; without a working foot valve the tube drains between pulses and each 1-min pulse (25 mL/min) only re-primes (zero dosing, 2026-09-26..29). Foot valve installed 2026-09-29 (Seko "válvula con contrapeso" is just a weighted strainer — blow test: air must NOT pass downward). The old PVC suction tube leaked air under the pump's weak vacuum although it held liquid when sucked by syringe — replaced; dosing confirmed 2026-09-29 15:27.
- Manual run: `acid_pump.sh on` … `off` (in the mosquitto container) with the filtration pump running; selector to "Agua (lavado)" when flushing with water so the model does not count acid; back to "HCl 22% (salfumán Josmi)" after.
- Diagnostic ladder when pH does not respond (always restate the geometry above when you explain it: the owner forgets it and it decides the test order): (0) verify the premise: count acid pulses and plot pH per hour; if every pulse produces a 0.02–0.03 dip and the reading sits at max+0.05..0.10 there is no fault — go to TA instead; (1) pH trace around a 3–5 min run; (2) discharge test: disconnect the discharge tube at the injector into a cup → 25 mL/min expected; (3) nothing out → suction: bubbles in the drum = reversed head, tube leak at fittings, worn Santoprene tube, foot valve blocked/inverted, drum empty; (4) liquid out but no pool effect → injector check valve scaled (soak in 1:5 HCl) or probe/reading issue.
- Changing acid type: flush the line with water 2 min (selector "Agua") before switching between H₂SO₄ and HCl.
- Detector `binary_sensor.pool_ph_not_responding`: ≥ 6 ppm TA destroyed in 24 h AND pH ≥ max + 0.05 AND 24-h pH minimum ≥ max + 0.05 (delay 15 min on / 60 min off). `binary_sensor.pool_ph_pump_timeout` = controller code 3.

## pH maximum autoregulated by LSI (D15, 2026-10-05)
Because TA can only be corrected slowly and CH is high, the controller pH max is adjusted automatically so the live LSI stays near `input_number.pool_lsi_objetivo` (0.0): recommended pH max = pH_now − (LSI_now − objetivo) − 0.05 (the loop holds the water at max…max+0.1, hence the −0.05; the pH reading cancels out algebraically, only TA/CH/T/CYA matter), clamped to [`pool_ph_max_min` 7.20, `pool_ph_max_max` 7.70], 0.05 steps, hysteresis 0.10, at most one change every 6 h (`pool_ph_max_horas_min`), only with the pump running ≥ 10 min with flow, ≥ 2 h after any TA baseline/bicarbonate change (unmixed model), never upward while a dosing fault is flagged (`pool_ph_pump_timeout`, `pool_ph_not_responding`, alarm code 3), outside the 09:48–09:56 and 17:22–17:36 write windows, and only while `counter.pool_np_writes_hoy` < 6 (reserve for the plan writes). Written with `NPpHMax x.xx` (two decimals accepted and echoed by the controller, verified 2026-10-05; no NPSave) by `script.pool_ph_max_aplicar`, verified via `sensor.piscina_aquarite_plus_neopool_ph_max`, logged in the historial and notified (tag `pool_ph_max`). Examples: TA 42 → 7.70; TA 64–70 → 7.45. `sensor.pool_ph_max_recomendado` exposes `motivo`, `lectura_fiable`, `fallo_dosificacion`. Switch off `input_boolean.pool_ph_autorregulado` to set it by hand. Package: `packages_pool_lsi.yaml`.
